Are you a nonprofit serving Anna and the surrounding area? Partner with Mission Men of Anna — it’s free.

Why partner with us

  • Get found by people who need you. Your free profile appears in our “Need Help?” directory, where neighbors search by what they need and where they live.
  • Raise money together. We organize events — car & bike shows, cook-offs, raffles — and give the proceeds to local causes like yours.
  • Extra hands. Our members show up to help at drives, giveaways and community days.

How it works

  1. Create a free nonprofit login with your organization’s EIN.
  2. Confirm your email, then fill out your profile: what you offer, who you serve, hours, and what people should bring. You choose which details are public.
  3. We confirm your 501(c)(3) status with the IRS — usually within a few business days — and your profile goes live in “Need Help?”.
