It all started with one simple text.

On April 18, 2024, a text — “Wanna get a 3N beer tomorrow?” — turned into a regular meetup of eight men from different backgrounds at 3 Nations Brewery in Anna, and then into a brotherhood with a mission to serve. We became an official nonprofit on February 15, 2025.

Our mission is “to come together as men in service of those in need by organizing events to provide essential resources to support the community and the people of Anna.”
